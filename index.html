Chapter 01 / 04 · The Breaking

The moon broke.

One night the moon cracked along four seams. The shards drifted apart and stayed in the sky, and the sea stopped obeying a single pull. Four tides rose where there had been one.

Chapter 02 / 04 · The Drowned Harbour

The sea took everything.

Nerin was eight when the deep tide pulled her mother's ship under in sight of the dock. All the water gave back was a brass shard on a cord. She has worn it every day since.

Chapter 03 / 04 · The Reef Law

One law remains.

After the third war the four courts agreed on one rule. Any claim on the water is settled by two tidecallers on open reef, best of three falls. The loser's court gives up the sea.

Settle it on the reef.

Now the Abyss court has claimed Nerin's harbour, and their champion Oru has never fallen. She has never stayed down. She calls the storm and walks out alone.
