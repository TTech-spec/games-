The story so far

How the sea was divided

01

The Breaking

One night the moon cracked along four seams. The pieces drifted apart and stayed in the sky, and the sea below stopped obeying a single pull.

02

Four tides

Each shard raised its own tide. The storm tide came fast and loud. The still tide froze harbours overnight. The deep tide dragged ships under. The ember tide boiled up from the vents on the sea floor.

03

The courts

People who lived through a tide learned to call it. They formed four courts, built their cities on what the water left behind, and began to argue over every reef between them.

04

The Reef Law

After the third war the courts agreed on one rule. A dispute is settled by two tidecallers on open reef, best of three falls, and the losing court gives up the water. You are one of those two.
